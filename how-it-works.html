How drift is estimated

A plain-language guide to what this map shows — and what it does not.

When ashes are released on the water, they disperse and move with surface currents, wind, and tides. This site draws a gentle, estimated path from each release point forward in time — a way to imagine where Mom's journey may have carried her, not a precise GPS location.

What we use

  • The date and coordinates of each scatter
  • Typical surface current direction and speed for that sea region
  • A simple daily step model, recomputed when new drops are added

What we do not claim

  • Exact real-time location
  • Scientific survey accuracy
  • That the path follows a single ash particle

These paths are thoughtful estimates for remembrance, not exact locations. The sea keeps its own secrets.
